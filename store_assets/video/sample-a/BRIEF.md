## Intent

Sample A of three style samples for the Google Play preview video of the Halal Checker app
(com.fatihgulen.helalrehberi). "Clean & calm": real app screens inside a phone frame, the app's
own linen/emerald palette, slow transitions, short English text lines. Story: an unreadable
ingredient list -> scan -> instant verdict (HARAM / HALAL) -> the app reads several languages ->
logo. The user will compare it with Sample B (energetic, phone + tablet) and Sample C (LTX AI
intro shot + real screens) before the final ~30s video.

## Assets

- assets/screens/ - screen recordings/stills from the debug build seeded with the fictional
  "Vellmark" demo catalogue (app/src/debug/java/com/example/demo/DemoProducts.kt).
- assets/logo/play_store_icon_512.png - app icon (from design/play_store_icon_512.png).

## Customizations

- Language scene: the in-app language chip switching (EN / DE / FR / AR), same product read in
  several languages.

## Notes

- Fictional products only (brand "Vellmark"); no real brands, no Turkish products, no Turkish text.
- Google Play rules: no "free" / "no ads" / price claims, no "download now"-style calls to action.
  A subtle feature line such as "No account needed" is fine; keep "ad-free" out of the sample.
- Silent sample (no music); music comes with the final version.
