## Intent

Sample B, the energetic 30-second candidate for the Google Play preview video (user picked this
style over extending Sample A). Fast beat-synced cuts, kinetic type, phone and tablet together,
music-driven. Covers: the ingredient-label problem, scan -> verdict on the music drop, the three
verdicts, the madhhab setting (Hanafi turns whey chocolate halal with a sourced note), multi-language
results, history and the E-code guide, logo.

## Assets

- assets/bgm/track.m4a - ACE-Step 1.5 instrumental (seed 74, 118 bpm, drop at 8.25s), cut to 30s
  with a 1.8s fade-out. Generated locally (Wan2GP), model license MIT.
- assets/screens/ - phone + tablet captures of the debug build seeded with the fictional
  "Vellmark" demo catalogue.

## Notes

- Fictional products only; no real brands; no Turkish text or Turkish products.
